# Your first week on Poa

Follow a neighborhood group through a week of preparing a film night. Members join, find a venue, test the equipment, and choose what to watch.

Use the same sequence for your group's first event or project.

## Find your way around

Open the organization and visit the tools you need:

| Place | Try this first |
|---|---|
| Home | Add a description and the link to where your group talks. |
| People and roles | Check your first members and who can join. |
| Tasks | Add “Find a venue” or “Test the projector.” |
| Votes | Ask which film people want to see. |
| Treasury | Review the available funds if you plan to cover expenses. |
| Learn | Add a short guide if the education hub is enabled. |

## Invite your first members

Try the [joining steps](/docs/join#follow-the-joining-steps) with one other organizer first, including any fee or sponsorship prompt. Check the join page before sharing it. Can someone join directly, or do they need an invitation, an application, or vouches? If someone must respond to applications, decide who will do that.

For an open group, your message could be:

> We're starting a neighborhood film night. Join here to help choose the first film or take a task. Membership is open. We're looking for someone to help find a venue.

If your role requires two vouches, explain the extra step:

> Apply through this link and share your application with two eligible members for vouches. When the role becomes available, return to accept it.

Use the wording that matches your settings. The [joining guide](/docs/join) covers the different paths.

## Finish the first task

Make “Test the projector” specific: connect it, check the sound, and submit a note with anything the group needs to bring. Set its reward and identify who will review the result.

A member claims or applies for the task, does the test, and submits the note. Once an authorized reviewer approves completion, the task records the result and awards its configured reward. The organizer can see that the equipment is ready, and the member has a completed contribution to their name.

![Decentral Park task board with Open, In Progress, In Review, and Completed columns](/images/product/tasks-board.webp "Decentral Park: the Initial Basement Set-up project makes the work around a shared space visible.")

[The task guide](/docs/task-manager) explains rewards, funded bounties, and review.

## Choose the film and confirm the plan

Run a poll to choose the film. Explain who can vote and when voting ends, then complete the result-confirmation step when it is available.

A poll records a preference. To spend funds or change permissions, create a [binding proposal](/docs/hybridVoting) with the intended action. Check its execution after voting.

Before the screening, check the remaining tasks and share the final arrangements where your group talks. Afterward, invite another member to host and use the completed project to show them what organizing the event involves.
